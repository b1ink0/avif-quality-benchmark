Which AVIF quality looks as good as WebP 82?

WordPress saves AVIF at quality 82, which makes AVIF files larger than WebP at the same setting. Your results help pick a lower AVIF default that still looks as good.

  1. Drag the divider on the image to compare WebP (left) with AVIF (right).
  2. Move the quality slider to the lowest setting where the AVIF still looks as good as the WebP.
  3. Press Save and next. Skip any image you are unsure about.
  4. When you are done, press Finish and share. Sharing your results on the GitHub issue is optional.

Tips: use 1x zoom on a desktop screen if you can, and don't resize the browser to shrink the image. The quality number stays hidden on purpose so it doesn't influence you.
